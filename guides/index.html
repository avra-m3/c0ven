Writing a guide

A guide is one HTML page, linked above, in this folder. It names its goal in the title, lists what it needs, gives the steps in order, and ends when the goal is met. A step that needs a fact the reader may lack links the page in docs/ that holds it. The pages use style.css, whose colours are the logo's.
